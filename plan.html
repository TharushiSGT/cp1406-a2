Goal

  • Establish a welcoming and informative online presence for 100SMILES Dental Care.
  • Market professional dental services provided in the clinic by all ages.
  • Emphasize on the experienced and friendly staff of the clinic.
  • Provide easy access to information, contact, and booking options by patients.
  • Demonstrate the image of the clinic as providing friendly, trustworthy, and caring dental services.
  • Promote booking of appointments and availing of promotional offers to the potential clients.

Success Evaluation

The success of the website will be measured by:

  • A rise in the number of appointment inquiries via the contact page or email.
  • Favorable user response regarding design, clarity and ease of navigation.
  • Interaction with the content like articles and promotional offers.
  • Better internet presence of the clinic in terms of people searching dental services in Razole.
  • Unified branding and strengthening trust, professionalism and community care.

Target Audience

The target audience includes:

  • Families that would want a trusted, long-term dental clinic.
  • Parents bringing children for their first dental visit.
  • Grown-ups, and retired people who need continuous attention, such as check-ups and cosmetic dentistry.
  • People living locally in Razole and the surrounding neighborhood who appreciate quality and affordable dental services.

Such users will probably use the site using both mobile and desktop gadgets which means that a clean, accessible and responsive design is necessary.

Site Flowchart

The site structure will include the following pages:

  • Home Page – Overview of 100SMILES Dental Care, featured promotions, links to services, staff, and contact information.
  • About Us – Clinic history, mission, and team introduction (Dr. Johanna Grey, Dr. Benedict Sloan, Richard Attam, April Kepner).
  • Services – Preventive care, orthodontics, implants, cosmetic dentistry, and emergency care.
  • Articles / Resources – Informative articles on dental health such as "The Importance of Regular Dental Check-Ups" and "How to Choose the Right Toothbrush."
  • Contact / Bookings – Address, phone, email, opening hours, contact form, and map location (33 Fantail Street, Razole).
